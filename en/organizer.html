Logo PNG Logo AI 20th Anniversary PDF

The Korea Cultural Industry Forum (KCIF) is Korea's first purely private organization whose members are leading entrepreneurs, scholars, and experts in the Korean cultural industry. To contribute to the substantial development of an increasingly global and convergent cultural industry, KCIF hosts regular forums for discussion and works to develop policy alternatives and build infrastructure for the industry's growth.

The industrial value of culture continues to rise: film, music, drama, performance, and games already form enormous markets. To become an economic power, a nation must become a cultural power. KCIF also strives to build exchanges and networks across borders.
